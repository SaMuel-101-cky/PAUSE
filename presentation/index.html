01 / WALKTHROUGH

One decision, two effects

Acquire one second at a time. When stopping costs no more than continuing, emit the label, skip the rest of that epoch, and carry its endpoint into the next.

AT EACH PATCH

Compare two risks

Stop risk is the smallest expected class-weighted error cost under the calibrated posterior. Continue risk adds the cost of one more patch to a GP estimate of future risk. Stop at the first qualifying patch, or at 30 s.

BETWEEN EPOCHS

Carry only what was seen

At inference, the causal backbone uses the current prefix and each earlier stopping endpoint within the current block. It never sees the skipped suffix or future epochs. Memory resets every 32 epochs in the main evaluation.

DURING TRAINING

Vary the available history

The same EEG window is trained with full and randomly truncated historical endpoints. Both views still supervise every current prefix. This prepares the backbone for the histories generated by the stopper.

03 / DEPLOYMENT SCOPE

Three different quantities

Acquired EEG
The reported seconds count observed signal per 30 s epoch. At the first evaluated PAUSE point above the strongest ECTS peak, the baseline used 6.0–6.8 times as much EEG at its own peak operating point.
Front-end power
The analog front-end savings are estimated from datasheet active and standby powers and the acquisition lengths. They do not measure end-to-end wearable energy or battery life.
Inference latency
On the tested Dimensity 9500 mobile host, single-thread CPU backbone inference at the 1 Hz patch cadence took 9.44 ms median and 18.80 ms p99 with scheduler assignment. This is the compute time for a patch, separate from the time spent acquiring it.
